I build AI systems and ask why they work.


I am a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer from Upper Chitral, Pakistan, with a B.Sc. in Software Engineering and over two years of industry experience in machine learning and operations.

My independent work centres on LLM-integrated applications, such as Meridian and Adlytix AI, and on a research question that grew out of them: does gathering context through conversation make multi-domain LLM generation more reliable and easier to interpret?

I also work on order-flow-imbalance signal research, where I validate pipelines on synthetic data before making claims, and on classical NLP pipelines. I am now pursuing graduate study abroad to take this research further.
